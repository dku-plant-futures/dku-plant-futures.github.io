Get ready to start the new year **with the planet in mind**! DKU’s **Plant Futures EcoFeb** is bringing you three weeks of exciting events focused on sustainability, ethical living, and environmental action. 🌍💚

## 📅 Week 1 (Feb 10th - 16th)

### 🍦 Free Oatly Ice Cream

**Date & Time:** Friday, 02/14, 9-11 AM\
**Location:** Performance Café\
Try out Oatly’s latest flavors **for free**! Leave a review on the Abillion app, and **donate \$1 to support animals**. 🐾

### 🎨 Sustainability Art Contest

**Duration:** Feb 10th - 23rd\
**Theme:** Animals and Nature\
**Prize:** 50 RMB\
Submit your artwork online before **02/23** to showcase your creativity and love for the planet!

## 📅 Week 2 (Feb 17th - 23rd)

### 🐱 Building Cat Houses

**Date & Time:** Monday, 02/17, 5-6 PM\
**Location:** A Lobby\
Join **PF x APA x Sustainable DKU** in creating warm homes for campus cats using **recycled cardboard & old clothes**! 🏠🐈

### 🌍 Food & Sustainability Festival

**Date & Time:** Friday, 02/21, 12-6 PM\
**Location:** CCT 3rd Floor Breezeway\
Enjoy **delicious plant-based foods**, learn about sustainable living, and listen to insightful presentations!

### 🎤 Speakers Series

**Date:** Friday, 02/21\
**Location:** ~CCT 3rd Floor Theatre~[Zoom](https://duke.zoom.us/j/6666529045)

- **[Maggie Baird](https://supportandfeed.org/about/)** (Founder of Support + Feed) – 9-10 AM\
  Maggie Baird is the visionary behind Support + Feed, an organization dedicated to combating the climate crisis and food insecurity by promoting a global shift to an equitable, plant-based food system.

- **[Peter Singer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Liberation_\(book\))** (Author of *Animal Liberation*) – 10-11 AM\
  Peter Singer is an Australian philosopher renowned for his book *Animal Liberation*, which is considered a foundational text for the animal rights movement.

- **谭小岛/Isla** (Founder of 小岛生活/Islelife) – 1-2 PM\
  Isla is an eco-entrepreneur and founder of Islelife, a startup that assists event organizers, such as concerts, in sourcing eco-friendly products.\
  *Plus, enjoy ********Free Oatly Drinks******** during Isla's session!*

### 🔥 PF x Debate: Are GMOs any good?

**Date & Time:** Friday, 02/21, 3-6 PM\
**Location:** CCT 3rd Floor Theatre\
Engage in a thought-provoking debate on the benefits and drawbacks of genetically modified organisms.

### 🎬 Eco-Documentary Screenings

**Location:** Library 2192

- *Seaspiracy* – Friday, 02/21, 6:30-8:30 PM\
  A groundbreaking documentary that exposes the environmental impact of the fishing industry.

- *Cowspiracy* – Sunday, 02/23, 6:30-8:30 PM\
  This screening includes a discussion led by Isla, focusing on the sustainability issues within the animal agriculture industry.

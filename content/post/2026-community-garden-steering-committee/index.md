We are pleased to announce the **2026 Community Garden Steering Committee (CGSC)** of Duke Kunshan University!
The new committee will officially take over responsibilities starting **November 1, 2025**.
Thank you to all who have supported the garden throughout the past year, and to the outgoing team for their dedication and care.

**Contact Information:**
* Microsoft Teams: [https://tinyurl.com/dku-garden](https://tinyurl.com/dku-garden)
* Email: [dku-garden@proton.me](mailto:dku-garden@proton.me)

Hi there!
My name is **Xinyao (Tacia) Gao**, a sophomore majoring in **Environmental Science** with a focus on **avian ecology**.
I have a broad interest in all kinds of wildlife, so I founded the **Wildlife Observers Society (WOS)** in 2024.
Our goal is to explore and document wildlife while connecting with a broader community and **respecting nature as it is**.
I’m also learning to tell the stories of wildlife **through an artistic lens that combines science and creativity**.
I look forward to more exchanges and inspiration!

**Thoughts and Ideas:**

* **Focus Area:** Boost student involvement in sustainability work.
* **Improvement Suggestions:** Integrate biodiversity themes into the garden’s planting and educational programs.
* **Interested Role:** Student communication and biodiversity promotion.

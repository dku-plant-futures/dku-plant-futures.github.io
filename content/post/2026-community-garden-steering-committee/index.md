We are pleased to announce the **2026 Community Garden Steering Committee (CGSC)** of Duke Kunshan University!
The new committee will officially take over responsibilities starting **November 1, 2025**.
Thank you to all who have supported the garden throughout the past year, and to the outgoing team for their dedication and care.

**Contact Information:**
* Microsoft Teams: [https://tinyurl.com/dku-garden](https://tinyurl.com/dku-garden)
* Email: [dku-garden@proton.me](mailto:dku-garden@proton.me)
